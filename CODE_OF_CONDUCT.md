# BeanScore Code of Conduct

_Last updated 29 September 2026._

## Why this exists

BeanScore is a small, one-person project — but if you're contributing code, sharing feedback, joining a group journal, or just hanging around wherever people talk about it, this is the short version of "how we treat each other here."

## The basics

- **Be kind.** Disagreements happen, especially over something as personal as taste in coffee. Keep it respectful.
- **Be welcoming.** Everyone starts somewhere — whether that's their first pour-over or their first pull request.
- **Give people the benefit of the doubt.** Most misunderstandings online are just that — misunderstandings, not attacks.
- **Respect people's data and privacy.** If you're in a shared journal with friends, that's their tasting notes and photos too — don't be weird about it.

## Not okay, ever

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks — publicly or in DMs
- Discriminatory jokes or language, in any direction
- Sharing someone else's private info without asking
- Deliberately disruptive behavior (spam, trolling, derailing conversations)
- Anything that would make someone feel unsafe or unwelcome

## If you contribute code or ideas

- Be constructive in code review — critique the code, not the person
- It's fine to disagree with a decision; it's not fine to be a jerk about it
- Assume good intent, and explain your reasoning instead of just saying "no"

## If something goes wrong

If someone's behavior crosses a line, or you're just not sure how to handle a situation, reach out directly:

**quinlansmiley@gmail.com**

Reports are taken seriously and handled privately. Since this is a one-person project, there's no big committee — just a real person who'll look into it and figure out the right next step, which might mean a conversation, a warning, or removing someone from the community if it's serious enough.

## Scope

This applies anywhere BeanScore lives — the app itself, shared journals, GitHub (if you're reading this there), social posts, and any other space where people are interacting around this project.

## One more thing

This is a coffee journal built by someone who loves coffee and wanted a good way to track it. The goal is for this to stay a genuinely nice corner of the internet. Thanks for helping keep it that way.
